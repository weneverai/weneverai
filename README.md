# Shih-Wen Huang 黃詩雯

**Artist-researcher · Interaction designer · Founder of Vibeweaving**

[Portfolio](https://shihwenhuang.com/) · [Vibeweaving](https://vibeweaving.com/) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/shihwen1212/) · [Contact](mailto:hello@vibeweaving.com)

I'm Wen, a Taiwanese artist-researcher and interaction designer working across generative systems, computational craft and human–AI interaction.

I build instruments and interfaces that make their rules available to the person using them: inspect a structure, try an alternative, interrupt a process, or decide what to keep. Weaving is both a material practice and a way of thinking about these systems—through repetition, tension, inherited knowledge and the choices that remain open while something is being made.

My practice moves between code, thread, clay, paper and sound. Through **Vibeweaving**, I bring that work into browser tools, material experiments and hands-on workshops.

## Research

I investigate **productive friction**: the moments when a person can inspect, question, adapt or refuse a system's proposal. Current questions include how source conditions travel with an image or pattern, what materials reveal that a screen cannot, and how craft can inform the design of generative interfaces.

**Passing: Where There Is No There There** follows what happens to attribution and declared terms when an image leaves the system that created it. It connects my research on image circulation with digital and physical bead-weaving studies.

Selected publication and recognition:

- **Expanded 2026 / ACM:** [*Lineage Canvas: Productive Friction in Born-Digital Heritage*](https://doi.org/10.1145/3840416.3840450), an art paper presented through the Expanded conference at Ars Electronica.
- **Falling Walls 2026, Art & Science shortlist:** [*Breaking the Wall of Cultural Flattening in AI*](https://falling-walls.com/breakthrough-shortlist-2026).

## Product design & teaching

Alongside my independent practice, I have designed native home-security experiences for **Verizon Home** and mobile eSIM journeys for **Global YO**. That work informs how I approach complex systems: make the important states understandable, give people useful choices, and connect each decision to what happens next.

My teaching includes a 36-hour research workshop on generative AI in fashion and textile creation with **Fu Jen Catholic University's College of Textiles and Clothing**.

**Education:** M.S. Information Experience Design, **Pratt Institute**.
